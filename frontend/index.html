About JobShield

Protecting job seekers from fraudulent postings

JobShield is an AI-powered system designed to detect fake job postings using machine learning and natural language processing techniques.It analyzes job descriptions, company information, and suspicious patterns to identify fraudulent listings with high accuracy.

The system provides real-time analysis, helping users quickly determine whether a job is genuine or fake. By focusing on key indicators and risk patterns, JobShield ensures safer job searching and prevents users from falling into scams.
